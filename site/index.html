Company Overview

Dengi Market A modern financial network with digital infrastructure and nationwide presence.

We lend against two types of collateral: jewellery (gold) and electronics (smartphones, laptops and other gadgets). Accepting electronics is a competitive advantage over most pawnshops in Kazakhstan, which work with jewellery (gold) only.

We help clients across the country get fast access to funds secured by their property, combining transparent terms, convenient service and modern digital solutions.

We are building a resilient, technology-driven business model that creates long-term value for clients, partners and investors.

Business Model

How the business works: a client pledges jewellery (gold) or electronics (smartphones, laptops, gadgets) as collateral for a short-term secured loan. The loan is sized against an appraised collateral value, repaid with interest, and the collateral is released on repayment.

1AppraisalIn-house scales, a gold purity test and online appraisal - unique on the market - disbursement averages 15 minutes.
2DisbursementLoan issued same-day at an LTV of up to 80% for jewellery and up to 60% for electronics.
3ServicingAverage term - up to 45 days for gold and up to 25 days for electronics, with an average ticket of $440 and $180 respectively.
4ResolutionRepayment with the collateral returned to the client, or disposal of unclaimed gold via auction sale or sale to a refinery.

Key Investment Attractiveness Factors

Established track record

Over 10 years of successful operating history in consumer lending. On the Kazakhstan market since 2015.

National scale

Over 300 branches across every region of the country - the largest pawnshop network, ranked in the TOP-4.

Collateral-backed lending

100% of loans are secured by collateral - jewellery (gold) and electronics. Pledged items are held in safekeeping at the pawnshop until the loan is fully repaid.

Diversified loan book

The portfolio is diversified both by collateral type - jewellery and electronics - and by loan size, reducing concentration risk and making the portfolio more resilient.

Stable financial performance

ROA - 24.3%, ROE - 47.3% for fiscal year 2025 - steadily rising profitability over the last three years.

Proprietary technology platform

An in-house development team, proprietary CRM, website and mobile app - built without outsourcing, scaling together with the branch network.

Low leverage, ample liquidity headroom

Equity-to-Asset ratio of 48.2% (covenant ≥20%), Net Debt-to-Equity of 0.85x (covenant ≤3.00) - as of 1 July 2026, both with a substantial buffer.

Proven international funding history

An impeccable reputation on the international Kilde investment platform: a B+ rating, $12.0M raised since 2023, and zero missed payments.

Risk Management & Regulation

The risk management system combines collateral-backed lending, collateral appraisal controls, portfolio diversification and internal review procedures within the applicable regulatory framework.

LicenceNo. 10.21.0017.L, issued 12.03.2021 by the Agency for Regulation and Development of the Financial Market (ARDFM) of Kazakhstan
RegulatorAgency for Regulation and Development of the Financial Market (ARDFM), Kazakhstan
Risk management proceduresLearn moreShow less
Lending model100% secured / collateral-backed
Appraisal methodAppraisal specialist (at collateral intake)
Portfolio concentrationDiversified across regions - in major regions the company operates its own jewellery stores with display windows and large retail space for selling jewellery (gold) and electronics.
Appraisal reviewAuditor / revisor (after collateral intake)
Credit risk controlsA proprietary Risk Management Methodology has been developed. The company has a dedicated Analytics and Risk department that applies scorecards to assess borrowers.
Other disclosuresOnline underwriting with additional checks, using modern collateral verification methods (when needed, at the point of collateral intake).
